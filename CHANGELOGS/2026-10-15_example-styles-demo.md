# Example: chat styles demo screen

## What
- `example/lib/styles_demo.dart`: `StylesDemoScreen` with a bottom navigation bar of three tabs (Smooth Silk, Minimal Clean, Thread Feed). The header menu button switches light / dark.
- `example/lib/demo_client.dart`: `DemoChatwootClient`, an offline client with a fixed conversation (text, link, 5-image gallery, single image, file, voice) that answers sent messages after a typing indicator.
- Home list entry "Chat styles (demo data)".

## Why
Check the chat UI without a server.
