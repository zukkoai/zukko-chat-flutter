# L10n review

## What
Reviewed all six translations in `chatwoot_l10n.dart` against English.

## Why
Correctness of wording, script and completeness.

## Corrected strings
- ru:
  - `poweredByText: 'Powered by' (left in English) -> 'Работает на'`
  - `voiceCancelHint: 'Смахните для отмены' -> 'Проведите для отмены'` (the usual "slide to cancel" phrasing)
- uzLatn, uzCyrl, tr, kaa: no errors found (`oʻ`/`gʻ` use U+02BB consistently; Cyrillic and Karakalpak letters correct).
- `poweredByText` stays English in uz/tr/kaa: brand-first word order ("Zukko tomonidan") does not fit a prefix.

## Other
- Locale resolution (`uz`, `uz`+`Cyrl`, `uz_UZ`, `ru`, `tr`, `kaa`, unknown -> en) is correct, no change.
- Test "styles strings follow the app locale when l10n is null" failed only because the test app lacked a Cupertino localizations delegate for `uz`; added `DefaultCupertinoLocalizations.delegate` to the test.
